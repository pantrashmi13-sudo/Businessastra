## 🔷 What is Bizastra?

**Bizastra** is a cloud-based, full-suite **Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) system** designed specifically for small and medium-sized businesses (SMBs) in Nepal and the broader South Asian market. It replaces the fragmented use of spreadsheets, paper ledgers, and disconnected software tools — bringing every critical business function into a single, intelligent, always-accessible platform.

The name itself says it all: *Biz* (Business) + *Astra* (Weapon/Tool in Sanskrit) — **your business weapon for strategy, elevation, and growth.**

Whether you run a trading company, manufacturing unit, retail store, or service business, Bizastra gives you real-time control over your entire operation — from purchasing goods to generating VAT-compliant reports — all from one place.

---

## ❓ Why Bizastra? Why Does This Exist?

Most small and medium businesses in Nepal manage their accounts using:
- Handwritten ledger books
- Local desktop-only software (like Tally) with no cloud access
- Multiple disconnected Excel sheets
- No real-time visibility into cash flow, stock, or receivables

The result? **Lost time. Wrong numbers. Missed tax deadlines. Inventory chaos. Slow decisions.**

Bizastra was built to solve all of that — in one place, in the cloud, accessible from any device, at any time.

## ✅ Core Features

### 1. 📊 Dashboard — Your Business at a Glance
- Real-time overview of Companies, Customers, Vendors, Items, Fixed Assets, and Bills
- Recent bill activity feed — see what's happening right now
- Quick-action buttons — create a new bill in one click
- At-a-glance financial pulse without opening a single report

### 2. 🏢 Master Data Management
Centralized management of all your business entities:
- **Companies** — manage multiple business entities under one account
- **Warehouses** — multi-location inventory management with full warehouse configuration
- **Customers** — full customer profile, contact, and transaction history
- **Vendors** — complete vendor database with purchase tracking
- **Inventory (Items)** — product catalog with categories, pricing, units, stock tracking, and **RAG (Rack, Aisle, Grid)** precise warehouse mapping
- **Fixed Assets** — register and track all capital assets (machinery, vehicles, equipment)
- **Fixed Assets Register** — depreciation tracking and asset lifecycle management

### 3. 🧾 Bills & Purchase Management
The complete purchase cycle from bill entry to payment:
- **All Bills** — searchable, filterable list of every purchase bill
- **New Bill** — create purchase bills with line items, taxes, discounts, and vendor details
- **Purchase Returns** — manage returned goods to vendors with proper debit notes
- **Delivery Challans** — generate and track delivery notes for goods dispatched
- **Consumptions** — internal stock consumption records for manufacturing/operations
- **VAT Register** — auto-generated, IRD-compliant VAT report with input/output tax breakdown

### 4. 🏷️ Sales Management
The complete sales cycle from invoice to collection:
- **Sales Invoices** — professional sales invoices with tax calculation
- **Sales Returns** — manage customer returns with credit notes

### 5. 💳 Receipt & Payment
- **Receipt Vouchers** — record money received from customers
- **Payment Vouchers** — record payments made to vendors or other parties
- Single unified view of all cash inflows and outflows

### 6. 🏦 Cash & Bank Management
Complete financial account management:
- **Bank Accounts** — track multiple bank accounts with full transaction history
- **Petty Cash** — manage day-to-day small expenses
- **Loans** — track loan accounts, disbursements, and repayments
- **Fund Transfers** — record transfers between accounts

### 7. 📚 Accounting & Reporting
Professional double-entry accounting built right in:
- **Chart of Accounts (COA)** — fully customizable account structure (Assets, Liabilities, Income, Expense, Equity)
- **Journal Entries** — manual journal posting with debit/credit balancing
- **General Ledger** — detailed transaction history per account with running balance
- **Ledger Reports** — account-wise statement with date-range filtering

### 8. 🌐 Knowledge Graph & Document RAG — Unique Data Intelligence
A one-of-a-kind feature not found in any other SMB ERP:
- **Schema Map** — visual interactive map of how all data entities relate to each other
- **Data Explorer** — browse live business data (companies, customers, bills, items) as an interactive visual graph
- **Document RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)** — upload your business policies, manuals, and documents directly to your secure knowledge base. Our AI automatically chunks, embeds, and searches these documents so you can "chat" with your own business policies instantly.
- One-click audit presets: *Overdue Debtors*, *Cash Flow Trail*, *Inventory Map*
- Node pinning, zoom/pan, path tracing between entities
- SVG export for reports and documentation
- Mobile-optimized card explorer for on-the-go data browsing

### 9. 🤖 AI Chat Assistant
- Chat directly with your ERP data (customers, bills, inventory)
- Connects automatically to the **Document RAG** knowledge base
- Live database connection status
